# CommandCenter - Quick Demo Guide

🔗 **Live App**: https://commandcenter-sand.vercel.app

---

## 🚀 What is CommandCenter?

A **Team Productivity & AI-Mentorship Platform** that helps teams:
- Track daily work with AI-powered analysis
- Collaborate on projects with intelligent task suggestions
- Gamify productivity with streaks and leaderboards
- Get unblocked faster with AI mentorship

---

## 📋 Quick Start (2 minutes)

### Step 1: Create Account
1. Go to https://commandcenter-sand.vercel.app
2. Click **"Create account"**
3. Fill in your details (any email works, no verification needed)
4. Click **"Create Account"**
5. You're in! 🎉

### Step 2: Try The Pulse (Daily Logging)
1. Click **"The Pulse"** in the sidebar
2. Write what you did today, example:
   ```
   Today I worked on the authentication system, fixed 3 bugs in the login flow, 
   and deployed the backend to production. Had some issues with CORS but resolved them.
   ```
3. Click **"Submit Entry"**
4. Watch AI analyze it instantly:
   - ✅ Summary generated
   - ✅ Sentiment score calculated
   - ✅ Key points extracted
   - ✅ Achievements highlighted

## 🎯 Key Features to Explore

### 1️⃣ **The Pulse** (Daily Logging)
- Write daily work logs
- AI analyzes and summarizes automatically
- Tracks your streak (consecutive days logged)
- Crypto-signed entries (immutable, tamper-proof)

**Try it**: Write 2-3 logs to build a streak!

---

### 2️⃣ **Teams** (Collaboration)
- Create teams for your projects
- Invite members by email
- Manage roles (Owner, Admin, Manager, Member)
- Track team activity

**Try it**: 
1. Click **"Teams"** → **"Create Team"**
2. Name: "Dev Squad", Description: "Our development team"
3. Invite your friend: `their-email@example.com`

---

### 3️⃣ **Projects** (AI-Powered Planning)
- Create projects with descriptions
- AI suggests tasks automatically
- Assign tasks to team members
- Track progress

**Try it**:
1. Click **"Projects"** → **"New Project"**
2. Name: "Mobile App", Description: "Build a React Native app"
3. Click **"Analyze with AI"**
4. Get instant task breakdown with priorities!

---

### 4️⃣ **Leaderboard** (Gamification)
- See team rankings by impact score
- Track streaks and total logs
- Compete with teammates
- Motivate consistent logging

**Try it**: Click **"Leaderboard"** to see your rank!

---

### 5️⃣ **SOS Hub** (Get Unblocked)
- Report blockers (technical issues, resource needs)
- Chat with team about problems
- Get AI suggestions for solutions
- Track blocker resolution

**Try it**:
1. Click **"Help"** → **"Report Blocker"**
2. Describe an issue
3. Get AI-powered solutions instantly!

---

### 6️⃣ **Goals** (OKR Tracking)
- Set company/team/personal goals
- Track progress automatically
- Link goals to projects
- Hierarchical goal structure

**Try it**: Click **"Goals"** → Create a goal!
